# Target cycle: 2026-2027 academic year ONLY

The 2026-2027 academic year = the **Fall 2026**, **Spring 2027** and **Summer 2027** terms
(also written `2026-27`, `AY 2026/27`, `FY27`, "entering class of Fall 2026").

Accept a value only when one of these holds:
- The page ties it to 2026-2027: e.g. "2026-2027 tuition", "Fall 2026 deadlines",
  "Spring 2027 start", "Fall 2026 entering class profile", "Class entering 2026".
- The page names no year at all (a standing fact such as "36 credit hours", "100% online").

Reject (label `cycle_match: "different"`) anything tied to another year:
- `2025-2026`, `2024-25`, `Fall 2025`, `Spring 2026`, `Summer 2026` -> previous cycle.
- `2027-2028`, `Fall 2027`, `Spring 2028` -> next cycle, even though the page is current.
- Class profiles "Class of 2025", "Fall 2024 cohort" -> old data, even if they are the newest numbers shown.
- Rankings dated 2024 or 2025 editions.
- Deadlines whose date has already passed for a term that is not in 2026-2027.

When a page shows several years side by side (a tuition table with 2025-26 and 2026-27 columns,
a deadline table for Fall 2026 and Fall 2027), take **only** the 2026-2027 column or row and quote it.

If the only numbers on the site are for another year, the field is **not found** for 2026-2027.
Never carry an older value forward, never adjust it for inflation, never estimate.
